              Screen lock

              After this long without use, Topo saves, closes the open network and covers the screen until it is unlocked. For this PC only.

              Unlock with the PIN, or by signing in to your Topo server again with a code sent to your email. Each wrong PIN says how many tries are left, and after 10 only the sign-in unlocks. With no server to sign in to, the PIN can be forgotten from the fifth wrong try, which Settings then says, and after 10 each further try waits longer.

              The lock stops someone using a screen left unattended. It is not encryption: the networks stay in this browser as before, and anyone who can use this PC's account can read them with the browser's own tools. Without a PIN or a sign-in it only hides the screen.
