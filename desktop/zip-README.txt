Topo, offline
=============

Topo documents a site's network: devices, cables, subnets, Wi-Fi, racks and
providers, with checks, reports and spreadsheets.

How to use it
-------------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (a USB stick works too).
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari (double-click it, or
   drag it onto a browser window).

No internet connection is needed. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Where your work is kept
-----------------------
Your saved networks stay in the browser you opened Topo with, for this
folder only. Another browser, another computer, or the website itself does
not see them. Use Export (a .nettopo.json back-up) to move or keep a copy,
and keep the network you are working on before you close the tab.

Good to know
------------
- Share links and the QR codes on printed labels point at the website,
  https://topo.carino.systems/, because a file on this computer cannot be
  opened from anyone else's.
- The "Topo" name in the top bar opens this folder rather than the editor.
  Use your browser's Back button, or open index.html again.
- Phones and tablets: use the website, https://topo.carino.systems/ .
  iOS Files and Android cannot run a local index.html.
